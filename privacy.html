Spino Ghost Racing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. Spino Ghost Racing runs entirely on Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit). All data the app stores lives inside Reddit's infrastructure; the developer operates no external servers or databases.

What the app stores

  • Gameplay data: your Reddit username, avatar URL, race times, ghost replay inputs, leaderboard positions, championship entries, and tracks you publish.
  • Sponsorships: purchase records (order id, chosen cells, duration), your username and avatar URL, and any custom text or image you submit for display.
  • Moderation data: reports, flags, bans, and approval decisions made by subreddit moderators.
  • Engagement signals: if you arrive via a Reddit ad, the app may report an anonymized engagement event (hashed identifiers only) to Reddit's own advertising measurement system. No data leaves Reddit's platform.

What the app does not do

  • No tracking cookies, no third-party analytics, no advertising networks.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone outside Reddit's platform.
  • No collection of email addresses, passwords, payment details, or off-Reddit identity. Payments are processed entirely by Reddit; the developer never sees your payment information.

Data removal

Deleting a track removes its leaderboards, replays, and sponsorships. To request removal of other data connected to your username, message the moderators of the subreddit where the app is installed or the developer via Reddit.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
