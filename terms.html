Track sponsorships (paid feature)

Track sponsorships are optional, cosmetic digital goods purchased with Reddit Gold through Reddit's payment system. A sponsorship displays your Reddit username and avatar (and, optionally, custom text or an image) on a chosen track for a fixed duration (7 or 30 days). Sponsorships:

  • are cosmetic only and never affect gameplay;
  • expire automatically at the end of their duration;
  • always display your Reddit username for attribution;
  • may include custom text or images only after moderator approval. Until approved, a neutral placeholder with your username is shown. If your content is rejected you keep the sponsorship slot and may submit different content;

  • are otherwise subject to Reddit's payment and refund policies — refund requests are handled through Reddit Help, not by the developer.

If a track is deleted, associated sponsorships end with it.
