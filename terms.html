Track sponsorships (paid feature)

Track sponsorships are optional, cosmetic digital goods purchased with Reddit Gold through Reddit's payment system. A sponsorship displays your Reddit username (and, optionally, custom text or an image) on a chosen spot of a track. Regular track sponsorships run for a fixed duration shown at purchase (currently 3 or 7 days); championship stage sponsorships run until the championship ends. Sponsorships:

  • are cosmetic only and never affect gameplay;
  • expire automatically — at the end of their duration, or when the championship they were bought for ends;
  • always display your Reddit username for attribution;
  • are screened automatically before purchase (including by an automated content-safety service) — content that fails screening cannot be bought and you are not charged. Content that passes appears on the track immediately;
  • must not contain profanity, harassment, third-party brands, logos, trademarks, advertising for outside products or services, or any content that violates Reddit's rules;
  • remain subject to human moderation after publication: subreddit moderators and the developer may reject or remove content at any time. If your content is rejected, a neutral placeholder with your username is shown for the remainder of the term. Removal for rule violations does not entitle you to a refund;
  • are otherwise subject to Reddit's payment and refund policies — refund requests are handled through Reddit Help, not by the developer.

Each track carries a limited number of sponsorships at once, so a track can be temporarily sold out. Subreddit moderators may disable new sponsorship sales on their subreddit at any time; existing sponsorships run out their term. If a track or championship is deleted, associated sponsorships end with it.
